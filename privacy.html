The short version

Probette collects no data. It never connects to the internet, so nothing you open can leave your device. There is no account, no tracking and no analytics.

Your files stay on your device

Probette reads the video, audio and image files you open, drop or share with it, on your Mac, iPhone or iPad, and shows what it finds. It never changes them and never sends them anywhere. Exports (text, JSON, CSV) are written only where you choose.

The list of recent files keeps bookmarks to them so they can be opened again; it never contains the files themselves. The Quick Look and share extensions read only the file you show them.

The apps have no network permission at all. The same holds for Probette on Android: it does not ask for internet access.
